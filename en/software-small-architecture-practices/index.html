Software for small architecture practices

In a small architecture practice the same person handles design, existing buildings, permit drawings and the images for the client. Amphion is made for exactly that: one program from the first idea to the finished drawing that takes the drafting off your hands.

Few people, many jobs

In a practice of one to three people there is no visualisation department and nobody who only draws. Every hour spent on lines is missing from the design and from the client. At the same time the expectations are the same as in a large firm: clean drawings, a model for the consultants, images for the client.

That is where Amphion comes in. You describe what you want in plain sentences, spoken or typed, and Amphion builds real walls, slabs, columns, windows and stairs with dimensions, layers and quantities. From them Amphion draws floor plans, sections, elevations and detail sheets. You check and decide.

What Amphion takes over in daily work

  • The first model. A sentence like “Two storeys, pitched roof, terrace facing south.” is enough to start.
  • The existing building. The 1913 villa with an extension, covering the existing building, design, interiors and floor plans, was modelled in about two hours.
  • The drawings. Floor plans, sections, elevations and detail sheets straight from the model, ready to print.
  • The hand-over. IFC, DXF, 3dm, STEP or PDF to structural engineers, building services and clients.
  • The images. Amphion renders with Blender in the background on your computer. You do not need to know Blender.

What it costs and when it pays off

The download is free, and you can try everything free for 30 days. To subscribe you add a card, and you can cancel any time before the first charge. For the work there are three plans, all prices plus VAT:

  • Amphion Manual, €49 a month: the complete program for manual modelling with building elements, imports, plans, sections, elevations, every export and every update; three requests a month to get to know the AI.
  • Amphion, €149 a month: everything in Manual plus the chat without a monthly limit on the strong models. Text or voice in, model and drawings out.
  • Amphion Team, €399 a month for three seats: Amphion for every seat, each additional seat €129 a month, one invoice.

Pay yearly and get two months free. Work it out with your own numbers: if Amphion saves you one or two hours of drawing, dimensioning and labelling a month, it has paid for itself. And if not, you cancel within the 30 free days and pay nothing. You can cancel at any time with an email.

Your data stays in the office

Projects and models live on your computer. Only what you write in the chat goes to the AI. You do not need your own AI key; the AI is included in the subscription. Amphion runs on the Mac from macOS 14 and on Windows 10 and 11; the licence key is for one computer and can be moved at any time under “My account”.
