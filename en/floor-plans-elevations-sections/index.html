Create floor plans, elevations and sections

In Amphion you do not draw floor plans, elevations and sections next to the model, you draw them from it. When the model changes, the drawings change with it. As many sheets as you need, ready to print for design, existing buildings and permit drawings.

Drawings from the model, not next to it

If you draw floor plans, elevations and sections separately, you maintain three versions of the truth. When a wall moves in the plan, someone has to remember to update the section and two elevations as well. In Amphion there is only one truth: the model of walls, slabs, columns, windows and stairs. Floor plans, sections, elevations and detail sheets are drawn from it.

You can build the model in two ways. You describe it in the chat, for example “Two storeys, pitched roof, terrace facing south.”, or you model by hand with snapping, dimensions and levels. Both can be mixed.

How a drawing sheet comes about

  1. Build the model. Storeys, walls, openings, stairs and roof, in the chat or by hand.
  2. Choose section and elevation. Say where to cut and which facade you want to see, for example “Cut at 60 metres”.
  3. Set up sheets. Floor plans, sections, elevations and detail sheets, as many sheets as you need.
  4. Complete and export. Add dimensions, labels and hatching as in CAD. Then export the sheets as PDF, or the model as DXF and IFC.

Single requests work in the chat too: “Cut open the ground floor” or “First floor from above, axonometric”. The view follows the sentence; the model stays the same.

What goes on the drawings

  • Floor plans for every storey, with walls in their build-ups, openings, stairs and dimensions
  • Sections through the building, wherever you want to cut
  • Elevations of every facade, axonometric views as well
  • Detail sheets for the places that need a larger scale

Because the building elements carry dimensions, build-ups, layers and quantities, the drawings are more than lines. Consultants receive the model as IFC or DXF with layers; the client receives the PDF.

For design, existing buildings and permits

The drawings are meant for all three. In design you draw variants quickly. For an existing building you model the house as it is and draw survey drawings from it; the conversion of a 1913 villa with three floor plans was modelled in about two hours. For the permit you add dimensions and labels and export sheets ready to print.

Getting started takes three steps: download Amphion, enter your licence key on first launch and open an example. The example shows straight away how floor plans, sections and elevations come from a single model. Amphion runs on the Mac from macOS 14 and on Windows 10 and 11. Projects and drawings stay on your computer; only the text of your chat goes to the AI.

Questions

Which drawings does Amphion produce?

Floor plans, sections, elevations and detail sheets, as many sheets as you need, straight from the model and ready to print.

Do the drawings update when I change the model?

Yes. The drawings are drawn from the model. A wall you move then sits in its new place in the plan, the section and the elevation.

Can I also draw by hand?

Yes. By hand you draw with snapping, dimensions and hatching as in CAD. Levels and every tool for manual modelling are included.

In which formats can I hand over the drawings?

As PDF for printing and for the client, as DXF with layers and as IFC for consultants. The model can also go out as 3dm or STEP.

Is it enough for a planning or building permit?

Amphion is made for design, existing buildings and permit drawings and produces sheets ready to print. Whatever your authority asks for in detail, you add as usual with dimensions and labels.
