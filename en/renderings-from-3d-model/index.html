Architectural renderings from your own 3D model

A rendering usually needs an export, a second program and someone who knows how to use it. In Amphion the images come from the same model as your drawings. Amphion renders with Blender in the background on your computer, and you only see the image.

One model for drawings and images

The usual route to an architectural image is a detour: export the model, open it in a rendering program, reassign the materials, set up the light. If the design changes afterwards, it all starts again. In Amphion the model stays where it is. Walls, slabs, windows and stairs carry their dimensions and materials, and exactly this model produces the floor plans, sections, elevations and renderings.

The same goes for interiors. Because the building elements carry dimensions and materials, the image shows what you have built: the windows, the ceiling height, the floor. For an image for the client you do not rebuild anything, and whatever is wrong in the image you change in the model. That way the rendering shows exactly the building that is also on the drawings.

How a rendering comes about

  1. Build the model. In the chat, for example “Two storeys, pitched roof, terrace facing south.”, or by hand.
  2. Choose the view. Turn the model to the view you want to show, or say it: “Show me the garden” or “From above, axonometric”.
  3. Render. Amphion renders with Blender in the background on your computer. You do not need to know Blender or export anything.
  4. Change and render again. You change the model, the drawings follow, and the next image shows the new state.

The rendering is no longer a project of its own but one more view of the same model. If you show three variants in a design, you render three variants without maintaining three files.

What the images are for

  • For the client. An image says more than a floor plan, especially for interiors.
  • For competitions and boards. For the waterfront tower, model, drawings and boards came from Amphion.
  • For your own design. If you see light and space early, you decide earlier.

Rendered on your computer

Rendering happens locally. Your projects and models stay on your computer and are not uploaded for an image. Only the text you write in the chat goes to the AI. Amphion runs on the Mac from macOS 14 with Apple Silicon or Intel and on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).

Questions

Do I need to know Blender?

No. If you want images, Amphion renders with Blender in the background on your computer. You only see the image.

Do I have to export my model for the rendering?

No. The rendering comes from the same model as your drawings. You only export when consultants continue working on the model, for example as IFC or DXF.

Is my model uploaded for the rendering?

No. Rendering happens on your computer. Projects and models stay local; only the text of your chat goes to the AI.

What happens to the image when I change the design?

You change the model and render again. Drawings and images come from the same model and show the same state.

Does it work for interiors?
