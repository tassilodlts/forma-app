Architecture software for Mac

Amphion is architecture software for Mac and Windows, made for architects running their own practice. You describe what you want to build, Amphion builds the model from real building elements and draws the plans from it. From the first idea to the finished sheet in one program.

One program for the whole journey

Many practices on Mac or PC work with a chain of programs: one for the sketch, one for the model, one for the drawings and another for the images. Amphion brings that chain together. The model is made of walls, slabs, columns, windows and stairs with dimensions, build-ups, layers and quantities. From exactly this model come floor plans, sections, elevations and detail sheets, as many sheets as you need.

The difference is how you work. You say or type in plain sentences what you want, for example “Two storeys, pitched roof, terrace facing south.” Amphion builds, you adjust, then you export the drawings as PDF. The microphone is always right in the input line. If you prefer to work by hand, you have snapping, dimensions, levels and every tool for manual modelling.

Made for Mac and Windows

Amphion runs on any Mac with macOS 14 or later, on Apple Silicon and on Intel. The Mac version is notarised by Apple, so it opens without detours. Amphion runs just the same on any PC with Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), with the same features. A practice with Macs and PCs side by side works with the same files.

Your projects live on your computer, not in someone else’s cloud. The chat needs an internet connection; only the text you write goes to the AI. Models and drawings stay local.

How to start

  1. Download Amphion. On the Mac, drag the app into the Applications folder; on Windows, run the installer.
  2. Enter your key. On first launch, enter your licence key and open an example.
  3. Start the way you like. Describe your building in a sentence or model by hand. Then draw the plans and export them as PDF.

What you end up with

  • A model of building elements with dimensions, storeys, layers and quantities
  • Floor plans, sections, elevations and detail sheets, ready to print, for design, existing buildings and permit drawings
  • Hand-over to structural engineers, building services and clients as IFC, DXF, 3dm, STEP or PDF
  • Images from the same model: Amphion renders with Blender in the background on your computer, and you only see the image

An example from practice: the conversion of a 1913 villa with an extension, covering the existing building, the design, the interiors and the floor plans, was modelled in about two hours. The waterfront tower on our home page is made of 69,510 building elements; model, drawings and boards come from Amphion.

Questions

Which computer do I need for Amphion?

A Mac with macOS 14 or later, with Apple Silicon or Intel, or a PC with Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). The chat needs an internet connection.

Does Amphion replace my CAD program?

For design, existing buildings and permit drawings, yes. Amphion draws plans, sections, elevations and details from the model, ready to print. By hand you draw with snapping, dimensions and hatching as in CAD. Consultants receive the model as IFC or DXF with layers.

Can I switch between Mac and Windows?

Yes. It is the same software with the same files. The licence key is for one computer; you can move it at any time under “My account”.

Where are my projects stored?

On your computer. Projects and models stay local. Only the text of your chat goes to the AI.

How do I cancel?

With an email, at any time. The subscription runs until the end of the term. If you are not happy in the first month, you get everything back. Your files stay with you.
