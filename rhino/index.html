Amphion for Rhino at a glance

Amphion for Rhino is an AI modeling plugin for Rhino 7 and 8 on Mac and Windows that turns text instructions into real, editable Rhino geometry (massing, walls, slabs, roofs, stairs and openings) and renders the current view as a photorealistic image. It also makes 3D print (STL, 3MF) and laser cut files (DXF, SVG) with Amphion Model Making. Version 1.1.0. It is free for 5 edits and 3 renders without an account, then costs 0.25 € per edit in Standard mode, 0.90 € per edit in Precise mode (Claude) and 0.25 € per render, or 12 € a month with your own Claude or OpenAI key and no fee per edit.

1Describe“A 5 storey block, 18 x 12 m, 3 m floors, gable roof.” Or select objects and say what to change.
2Amphion buildsIt reads your scene, plans in real dimensions and builds solids on clean “Amphion” layers, in your document units.
3It checks its workAmphion looks at the viewport, fixes what is off and tells you floors, area and height. One click undoes the whole edit.
4RenderTurn the current view into a photorealistic image that keeps your exact geometry and camera.

Things you can ask

  • “Build a 3 storey residential block, 20 x 12 m, with 35 cm exterior walls, slabs and a gable roof.”
  • “Add balconies 1.5 m deep to every floor of the south facade.”
  • “Cut 1.2 x 1.4 m windows every 3 m into the selected wall, sill at 0.9 m.”
  • “Array these columns on a 6 m grid, 5 by 3.”
  • “Make a stair from ground floor to first floor next to the entrance.”
  • “Put everything on proper layers and name the floors.”

Install

Recommended on Mac and Windows: the package amphion3d-1.1.0-rh7_0-any.yak. Do not double-click a downloaded .rhp on a Mac: Rhino 7 then reports it as damaged.

Mac and Windows, Rhino 7 or 8 (recommended)
  1. Download amphion3d-1.1.0-rh7_0-any.yak.
  2. Drag the file onto the open Rhino window. Rhino's package manager installs it and keeps it loaded. Restart Rhino if the command is not found.
  3. Type Amphion in the command line to open the panel, or AmphionAsk to ask from the command line.
Alternative: the .rhp file
  1. Download Amphion.rhp.
  2. Do not open it with a double-click. In Rhino use Tools › Options › Plug-ins › Install (Mac: Rhino › Settings › Plug-ins › Install) and choose the file, or drag it onto the open Rhino window.
  3. Type Amphion to open the panel.

Questions

Does it change my model without asking?

Amphion only touches what you ask for. New geometry goes on layers under “Amphion”. “Undo last edit” in the panel removes everything the last request did, and Rhino's own Undo works step by step.

Which AI does it use?

Amphion picks a strong model for modeling with tools. With your own key it uses Claude (Anthropic) or OpenAI directly, billed to your account.

Is my model sent anywhere?

Only what the agent needs: a list of objects with layers and sizes, and a viewport image when it checks its work or when you render. Your file is never uploaded and nothing is used for training.

What about Revit, SketchUp or Archicad?

Amphion's own app does plans, sections and BIM export. See Amphion.
