Model making from Rhino: 3D print and laser cut files in one click.

Amphion Model Making is a plugin for Rhino 7 and Rhino 8 on Mac and Windows that turns architectural models into fabrication files. AmphionPrint scales the selection to 1:50, 1:100, 1:200, 1:500 or any scale, makes it one watertight solid with the minimum wall thickness of your printer and exports STL and 3MF. AmphionLaser makes contour layers, unrolled facades with openings or floor plates, nests the numbered parts on your sheet size and exports DXF and SVG per sheet. 3 exports are free, then 4 € a month. Download: Amphion.rhp.

Laser cut architectural models from Rhino

Select the model, run AmphionLaser, pick scale, material and sheet. Amphion does the tedious part:

  • Contour model laser cut: terrain or massing sliced at the material thickness at scale (3 mm board at 1:200 = one layer every 0.6 m), the next layer scored on each part for gluing.
  • Unroll facade for laser: every planar wall face laid flat with window and door openings as holes; reveals and recess backs are left out.
  • Floor plates: one section per storey at your floor height.
  • Nesting: parts placed on 600 × 400, 800 × 500, 1000 × 600 mm or custom sheets, rotated 0° or 90°, 3 mm apart.
  • Laser ready: kerf compensation, engraved part numbers, cut red (255,0,0), engrave blue, score black, 0.01 mm hairlines. One DXF and one SVG per sheet, and the sheets 1:1 on layers Amphion::Laser in your file.

3D print architecture models with the right wall thickness

Most failed architecture prints are walls that are too thin at scale or models that are not watertight. AmphionPrint checks both before you slice:

  • Copies and scales the selection to millimetres at 1:50 to 1:500.
  • Caps planar holes, thickens open surfaces, joins everything with a boolean union into one watertight solid.
  • Minimum wall at print scale: FDM 0.8 mm, SLA 0.5 mm, powder 1.0 mm. Thinner parts are thickened or marked.
  • Finds open and non-manifold edges and marks them as dots on Amphion::Fab::Problems.
  • Optional base plate, optional split into floors for stacked models.
  • Exports binary STL and 3MF plus a report with size in mm and volume in cm³.

Install

  1. Download Amphion.rhp (Rhino 8 also: amphion3d-1.1.0-rh7_0-any.yak).
  2. Drag it onto the open Rhino 7 or 8 window. On Windows you can also use Tools › Options › Plug-ins › Install.
  3. Select objects and type AmphionPrint or AmphionLaser. For scripts: -AmphionLaser Mode=Contours Scale=200 Thickness=3.

The same plugin contains the Amphion AI modeling agent for Rhino.

FAQ

How do I make laser cut files for an architectural model in Rhino?

Install Amphion for Rhino, select the model and run AmphionLaser. Choose the scale, material thickness, sheet size and kerf, and a mode: contour layers, unrolled facades or floor plates. Amphion lays the numbered parts out on sheets and writes one DXF and one SVG per sheet, with cut lines red, engraving blue and score lines black.

What minimum wall thickness does a 3D printed architecture model need?

At print scale, about 0.8 mm for FDM, 0.5 mm for SLA resin and 1.0 mm for powder printers. A 10 cm wall at 1:200 is only 0.5 mm, so it fails on FDM. AmphionPrint measures every part at print scale, thickens parts below the minimum or marks them on the layer Amphion::Fab::Problems.

How do I make a contour model for laser cutting from a Rhino terrain?

Select the terrain surface, mesh or solid and run AmphionLaser in Contours mode. Amphion slices it at the material thickness converted to model scale, so 3 mm board at 1:200 gives one layer per 0.6 m of height. Each layer gets the outline of the next layer as a score line for gluing.

Can I unroll facades with window openings for laser cutting?

Yes. In Facades mode Amphion lays every planar wall face of the selected polysurfaces flat, keeps the window and door openings as inner cut lines, skips reveals and recess backs, and names each part with its number and orientation, for example F1 S.

Which file formats does Amphion Model Making export?

STL (binary) and 3MF in millimetres for 3D printing, plus one STL per floor when the model is split. DXF (R12, layers CUT, ENGRAVE, SCORE) and SVG (millimetres, 0.01 mm hairlines) per sheet for laser cutting.

Does Amphion Model Making work in Rhino 7 on Mac?

Yes. It runs in Rhino 7 and Rhino 8 on macOS and Windows. It is part of the Amphion for Rhino plugin (Amphion.rhp).

How much does Amphion Model Making cost?

3 exports are free without an account. After that, unlimited exports cost 4 € a month or 40 € a year, plus VAT. An Amphion licence includes Model Making.
